Introducing

The 5 Activators

There are 5 Agreements that make someone want to buy from you.

But even when someone agrees with all five, they can STILL put off buying.

Why?

Because there's another side of buying psychology that influences whether someone acts today or decides to wait.

I call these The 5 Activators.

  1. 01

    Cost Awareness

    They recognise the real trade-offs of putting the decision off.

  2. 02

    Decision Certainty

    They feel clear and confident enough to make the investment.

  3. 03

    Execution Confidence

    They believe they can realistically implement what they're buying.

  4. 04

    Reward Proximity

    They can see meaningful progress beginning sooner than they thought.

  5. 05

    Opportunity Alignment

    There's a genuine reason this particular opportunity feels right for them NOW.

And here's the important part…

You don't need to use aLL five every time you seLL.

You need to understand which ones your buyers might be missing, and how to solve them through your content, stories, emails and offers.

So instead of creating more desire in people who are already sold on you…

You can start giving them more reasons to act — now.
