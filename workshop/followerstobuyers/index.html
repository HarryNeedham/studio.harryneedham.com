The Framework

The 4D's

A simple 4-part process to figure out what makes your audience buy, why it's not happening more often, and how to start changing that.

01

Drivers

Understand what actually gets people to buy.

Why does someone who's followed you for 6 months suddenly decide TODAY is the day they want to work with you?

It's rarely random.

There are certain problems, desires and moments that push people from "I love your stuff" to "How can I work with you?"

We'll break down what actually creates those moments, so you can stop waiting for people to magically become ready.

02

Data

Find out what's already made your best clients buy.

Chances are, you've already created content, made offers and said things that have brought you some amazing clients.

But do you actually know what made them buy?

We'll look back at your previous sales, enquiries, content and client conversations to find the patterns.

I'll also show you how I used AI to analyse 3 years of my own business data, and how you can do the same.

03

Disconnect

Find out why it's not happening more often.

Now we look at what you're currently doing, compared to what's actually brought you buyers in the past.

Maybe the topics that get you the most enquiries are the ones you barely talk about.

Maybe you've got an audience that would happily buy from you, but you rarely give them an opportunity to.

We'll find out where you're leaving buyers on the table, and what needs to change to get them moving again.

04

Deploy

Turn what you've learned into a plan that gets buyers moving.

Now we put it all together.

I'll walk you through 5 of my favourite Profit & Pipeline Plays, showing you different ways to promote what you already sell and give your audience a genuine reason to act NOW.

Then we'll build your 14-Day Buyer Activation Plan, with the content, stories, emails and offers to run over those two weeks.
